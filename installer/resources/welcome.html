Asus Xonar Essence STX / STX II driver

Installs CMI8788Driver, a macOS port of the Linux snd-virtuoso driver, and the STX menu bar app for card settings that Sound preferences doesn't cover.

For Intel Macs and hackintoshes. The card's auxiliary power connector must be plugged in.

OpenCore users: on the next screen, click Customize and untick the driver; add the kext to your EFI instead (see the README).
