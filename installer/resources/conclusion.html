Installed

The STX menu bar app is running and starts at login.

If you installed the driver to /Library/Extensions: it loads when SIP's kext signing check is disabled. On Big Sur and later, approve it in Security & Privacy and restart.

OpenCore users: choose Show Kext for OpenCore… in the STX menu to find the kext.

To uninstall: sudo sh "/Library/Application Support/CMI8788Driver/uninstall.sh"
